Who We Are

Rooted in Nigeria,
Reaching the World

LOOK AGRIC ALLIED LIMITED is a Nigerian farm produce export company headquartered in Ibadan, Oyo State. We specialise in the sourcing, processing, and export of premium agricultural commodities — connecting Nigeria's richest farmlands to global buyers across Europe, Asia, the Middle East, and the Americas.

We work directly with trusted farmers across Nigeria's most productive agric belts, ensuring every lot meets international standards for quality, purity, and consistency — every shipment, every time.

Why Partner With Us

The LOOK AGRIC Advantage

We combine Nigeria's agricultural depth with the professionalism and reliability demanded by international buyers.

01

Direct Farm Access

We source directly from certified farmer cooperatives across Nigeria's prime agric belts, eliminating intermediaries and ensuring freshness and traceability from farm to port.

02

Stringent Quality Control

Every consignment undergoes rigorous grading, moisture testing, and visual inspection before loading. We don't ship until it meets international specification.

03

Complete Documentation

Phytosanitary certificates, fumigation reports, SGS inspection, Bills of Lading, certificates of origin — the full documentation package for seamless customs clearance.

04

Multi-Port Flexibility

We ship from Apapa Port, Tin Can Island, and Onne Port, offering both FOB and CIF incoterms. We coordinate freight forwarding for any global destination.

05

Dedicated Export Team

Every buyer gets a dedicated export manager for consistent communication. From first quote to port delivery, you'll always know exactly where your order stands.

06

Global Buyer Experience

We have handled enquiries from Europe, the Middle East, Asia, and the Americas. We understand international buyer expectations and exceed them on every order.
