Who We Are

Rooted in Nigeria,
Trusted Worldwide

LOOK AGRIC ALLIED LIMITED is a professional farm produce export company headquartered in Ibadan, Oyo State — the heart of Nigeria's most productive agricultural belt. We connect West Africa's finest cocoa, cashew, sesame, and shea growing zones directly to global buyers.

Every shipment we manage is backed by direct farm relationships, independent quality inspection, and complete export documentation — phytosanitary certificates, SGS reports, and all required customs paperwork. From your first enquiry to final delivery, you have one dedicated contact.

Why Partner With Us

The LOOK AGRIC Difference

We combine the depth of Nigeria's agricultural sector with the professionalism and responsiveness that international buyers deserve.

01

Direct Farm Access

We work directly with certified farmer cooperatives across Nigeria's prime growing regions — no middlemen, full traceability from farm to container.

02

Rigorous Quality Control

Every batch is graded, moisture-tested, and visually inspected before loading. We will not ship product that doesn't meet your specification.

03

Complete Documentation

Phytosanitary certificates, fumigation reports, SGS inspection, Bills of Lading, certificates of origin — everything you need for smooth customs clearance.

04

Multi-Port Flexibility

We ship from Apapa, Tin Can Island, and Onne ports — offering both FOB and CIF, with freight coordination to any destination worldwide.

05

Personal Export Manager

Every buyer gets a dedicated contact. From first quote to port delivery, you'll always know exactly where your order stands — no chasing, no confusion.

06

Internationally Experienced

We've shipped to buyers across Europe, the Middle East, Asia, and the Americas — and we understand exactly what each market expects in terms of quality, documentation, and communication.
